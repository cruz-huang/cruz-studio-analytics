Cruz Studio Analytics

这是视频频道「Cruz AI 路痴指南」作者自用的一个小程序。它在作者自己的电脑上运行，用 YouTube 官方接口读取作者本人频道的观看数据，用来复盘每期视频。

它不对外提供服务，也不读取任何其他人的数据。

Cruz Studio Analytics is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Cruz AI 路痴指南" (@CruzAIGuide). It runs on the owner's own computer and reads the owner's own channel analytics through the official YouTube APIs. It is not offered to anyone else.
